Squared curves and straight cuts, in four members.

Katagami Sans is a sans serif for interfaces. Katagami Round has geometric bowls and rounded vertices. Katagami Text is the reading member, smaller and softer. Katagami Mono is the monospace for code and terminals. All four share one skeleton: level cuts on c, e, s and a, a g tail that mirrors its bowl, and y and t tails that end in vertical cuts.

katagami (n.) a Japanese paper stencil, cut by hand, used to print patterns on cloth.

Katagami

Katagami (型紙, “pattern paper”) are stencils for dyeing cloth, cut in Shiroko and Jike in Suzuka, Mie, for more than five hundred years. Sheets of washi are bonded with kakishibu, a fermented persimmon tannin that turns them brown and tough. A cutter carves the pattern through the layers, and a fine silk net holds the loose pieces in place. Rice paste goes through the holes, the cloth is dyed, and the paste washes out to leave the pattern white. Type a word to cut it, then dye the cloth.

Curves

Sans, Round and Text start from the same Inter outlines and differ in how far each curve reaches toward its corner. A quarter arc sits in a box whose corner is where its two end tangents meet, and the handle fraction f is how far each handle reaches toward that corner. A circle reaches 0.552 of the way and Inter about 0.60. Sans reaches furthest, Round less far with every sharp vertex rounded, and Text stays close to Inter's curves and changes the proportions instead.

Katagami Round

The geometric member. It keeps Inter's proportions, spacing and weights, squares its bowls a little less than Katagami Sans, and rounds every outer vertex: stem ends, terminals and apexes. Katagami's figures, the compact f, a G with a spur and level cuts on c, e, s, a and g. Nine weights from Thin to Black.

Good type, plainly spoken.

Katagami Round is built for the places where a typeface should not be noticed first: dashboards, settings pages, long documents and the labels on buttons. It keeps Inter's coverage and features, and its weights line up with Katagami Sans.

Katagami Text

The reading member. Its x-height is 0.71 of the cap height (Inter's is 0.75), every glyph is 4 percent smaller on the same line height, the spacing is tighter, and round letters and capitals are wider. Nine weights from Thin to Black, with the heavy weights lighter than Inter's.

A closer c, a slanted t, a lower x-height.

Katagami Mono

Katagami Sans fitted to a cell 1280 units wide, 0.625 em. Letters fill the cell evenly with their stems held, m and w run nearly edge to edge, f, t and r reach out on their arms, and every character that could be mistaken for another has its own shape. Code ligatures for -> => != == and >= are opt-in through dlig, and every character keeps its own cell. Seven weights from Thin to Bold.
